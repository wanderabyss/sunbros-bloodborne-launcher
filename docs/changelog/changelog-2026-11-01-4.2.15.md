# 4.2.15 — Sojin Chikage, a twin-blade Chikage at the Bath Messengers (2026-11-01)

Built on 4.2.14.

## Why

A new weapon for players who want the Chikage's blood arts with two blades: the familiar katana in normal mode, and the Blade of Mercy's twin-blade moves, coated in blood, when transformed. It started as a stand-alone mod and was play-tested and rebalanced before it moved into the launcher.

## What it is

**Sojin Chikage** (双刃千景, "twin-blade Chikage") is an optional PC enhancement (`pc_sojin_chikage`, `BB_PC_SOJIN=1`), off by default. Like the other enhancements, it is made from your own game files at start; the release ships no game files.

- **Three weapons,** as the Chikage has: Sojin Chikage, Uncanny Sojin Chikage and Lost Sojin Chikage. Each takes that Chikage's stats, scaling, requirements, upgrade path and gem slots, +0 to +10.
- **Where:** the Bath Messengers in the Hunter's Dream sell each at +0 for 10,000 Blood Echoes, from the start of the game and at every price stage. With ShopEdit, they are in ShopEdit's shop instead.
- **Normal mode:** the Chikage's own katana moveset.
- **Trick mode (L1):** the Blade of Mercy's twin-blade moveset with a blood hit on every cut. The twin attacks keep 65 % of the Blade of Mercy's motion values, so their damage per second matches the Chikage's transformed form. The blades drain 0.4 % of your HP a second while drawn (the Chikage's drain is 0.85 %).
- **Blood Rhythm:** each twin-blade hit adds a stack, up to five: +4 % damage and +1 bleed per stack. The stacks drop 5 s after your last hit.
- **Blood Crescent:** the second R2 of the twin-blade combo throws a wave of blood about 8 m forward.
- **Bankai:** the transform attack into twin blades (R1, then L1 during the swing) costs 20 % of your current HP (nothing below 10 %), bursts blood around you, and for 30 s gives +15 % damage, more bleed, a small heal on every hit and double the drain.
- **HUD icons** show Blood Rhythm building, the full five stacks, and Bankai.
- **Co-op:** every player in a session needs it on.

## What changed

- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/sojin/`: the weapon's builder (`weapon.py`), its moveset, ability and behaviour-graph modules, and the save converter (`save_tool.py`).
- `scripts/enhancements.py`: the `pc_sojin_chikage` feature (about 15 s the first time; reused afterwards).
- `scripts/mods.py`: when a mod above it replaces the gameparam (ShopEdit) or an item text, the weapons are added to that mod's copy in a layer on top, and their shop rows go to the ranges the Bath Messengers' talk script on top opens. A mod that already has the weapons (the stand-alone Sojin Chikage mod) is used as it is.
- `run.py`: with the feature off and no mod providing the weapons, a save holding one gets its Sojin weapons turned into the Chikages they were made from before the game starts, with a backup beside the save folder.
- Launcher (Mods page) and the in-game overlay: the switch.
- `packaging/windows/package.sh`: the packaged runner bundles `copy`, `csv`, `datetime` and `glob`.
- `tests/test_sojin.py`.

## How it was tested

- Made from the 1.09 dump and checked row by row against its sources (the new rows only; every existing row unchanged). The moveset and model files are byte-identical to the play-tested stand-alone mod.
- In game: the Bath Messengers list all three at 10,000 from the start; buying Sojin Chikage took 10,000 echoes; a save holding the stand-alone mod's weapon loads it as Lost Sojin Chikage; the katana, the transform and the twin-blade chain play with blood.
- The merge into ShopEdit's gameparam keeps all of ShopEdit's rows and adds the three weapons to its shop.
- The save converter on a copy of a test save: backup, conversion, re-read checks, and nothing left on a second run.
- Not yet tested: co-op, and plunge attacks.
