# 4.2.14 — DLSS 4.5 and DLSS 5 side by side in the README (2026-10-30)

Built on 4.2.13. No code changes: the launcher and the game are the same as 4.2.13.

## Why

The README opened with a 4.2.4 clip of DLSS 5 alone, and its look doesn't suit everyone. Side by side, DLSS 4.5 and DLSS 5 show what each one does, and that DLSS 5 is optional.

## What changed

- `README.md`: the Gameplay section shows `docs/images/dlss45-vs-dlss5-4.2.13.webp` (1024 px, 10 FPS, 7 MB), linked to `bloodborne-4.2.13-dlss45-vs-dlss5.mp4` (3840 × 1080, 60 FPS, 30 s, 150 MB) on the v4.2.13 release.
- `docs/images/gameplay-4.2.4-dlss5.webp` is removed; the 4.2.4 MP4 stays on the v4.2.9 release.

## How it was recorded

Two scripted runs of 4.2.13 with the same steps and settings: 3840 × 2160 output, DLSS Performance, DLSS 2× frame generation at a 60 FPS base, Reflex, ray tracing High, HDR off for the capture. One run used the plain package (DLSS 4.5); the other used the DLSS 5 copy through ReShade. Each half is the first 10 s at the Grand Cathedral, Great Bridge and Upper Cathedral Ward. DLSS 5 ran at about 41 game FPS (lowest 26) and DLSS 4.5 at 60. Bloodborne slows its game speed with the frame rate, so longer clips drift apart.
