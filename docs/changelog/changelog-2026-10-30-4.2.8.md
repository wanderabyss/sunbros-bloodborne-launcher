# 4.2.8 — Co-op bells back to normal outside co-op (2026-10-30)

Built on 4.2.7. Launcher only; no game or renderer changes.

## Why

After a seamless co-op session, starting the game offline or online showed the bells as **?GoodsName?** with a blank icon, and the Beckoning Bell was gone. BBSC 0.0.2 gives every character a co-op kit made of goods rows that only BBSC fills in while it runs (Hunt Bell 4016, Kin Bell 4121, Bloodlust Bell 11201, Duel Bell 4313, Parting Blank 11251), and takes the vanilla bells out of the inventory (Beckoning Bell 200, Small Resonant Bell 205, Sinister Resonant Bell 225, Silencing Blank 111). Without BBSC those rows have no name, icon or use.

## What changed

- `bbport_coop.py`:
  - `KIT_TO_VANILLA`: each kit item and the vanilla item it replaces (the Duel Bell has none).
  - `vanilla_bells_in`: finds each kit item's inventory slot (16 bytes: slot id, `0xB0000000|id` handle, `0x40000000|id` item, count) and writes the vanilla item into it. The Duel Bell's slot, or a kit slot whose vanilla bell is already in the inventory, becomes an empty slot as the game writes one (slot id kept, handle 0, item -1, count 0). Quick-item slots point at slot positions, so they keep the bell.
  - `restore_vanilla_bells`: does that for every `userdata00NN` and `backup00NN` save, after copying the saves to `coop-backups` with `backup_saves`. Nothing is written or copied when no save holds the kit.
- `bbport_launcher_backend.py`: `play()` calls it for every launch without BBSC (offline and online; co-op passes its own environment) and logs the backup folder.
- `tests/test_coop.py`: `test_kit_bells_turn_back_into_vanilla_bells_outside_coop`.

## Checked

- The co-op tests pass (29).
- A copy of a real save after co-op: the five kit slots became Beckoning Bell, Small Resonant Bell, Sinister Resonant Bell, an empty slot and Silencing Blank, laid out byte for byte like the same bells in a save from before co-op.
- Not yet checked in game.
