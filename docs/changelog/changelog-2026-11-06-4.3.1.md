# 4.3.1 — NG+7 hammer: a Stormforged Kirkhammer for an Arcane build (2026-11-06)

Built on 4.3.0.

## Why

NG+7 world (4.2.21) puts enemies at NG+1's multipliers times the cycle table's 1.2 on health and attack, and the game's stat curve gives little past 50, so a 99 Arcane build gains little over Arcane 50 on Thunderwright's Kirkhammer. This adds a stronger hammer as its own switch, with that weapon left as it is.

## What changed

- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/ng7_hammer/` (new): `weapon.py` makes the Stormforged Kirkhammer +0 to +10 (ids 2070000-2071000, sorts 55480-55490): the Kirkhammer's rows with Thunderwright's Kirkhammer's moves, lightning, effects and model (variation 2060, model 1032) and that weapon's base attack times 1.2 (90 + 66 at +0, 180 + 132 at +10), the same Strength correction, Arcane correction 1.3 times as strong (78 at +0, 130 at +10) and the same requirements (Strength 16, Skill 10, Arcane 25). It has its own name and lore. The Bath Messengers sell it at +0 for 10,000 Blood Echoes, and it upgrades with the Kirkhammer's materials: a character's save holds each weapon's open gem slots, and a weapon obtained directly at +10 has them all closed, whatever its WeaponGenParam row says, so an earlier build that sold it at +10 could take no gems. `verify()` checks the new rows and that every other row and file is unchanged.
- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/enhancements.py`: `pc_ng7_hammer` (`BB_PC_NG7_HAMMER`, off by default), layer `ng7-hammer` above Hunter's Arsenal's: that gameparam and those item texts with the weapon added. Switching it on brings `pc_sojin_chikage` with it.
- `bloodborne_pc/launcher/bbport_launcher_win.py`: its entry in Built-in enhancements. `gpu/shim/bbport_settings`: the same in the overlay's list.
- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/mods.py`: a mod with its own gameparam or item text gets the weapon added too, after Hunter's Arsenal's two.
- `bloodborne_pc/run.py`, `scripts/ng7_hammer/save_tool.py`: with the switch off, one in a save becomes the Kirkhammer at the same level, even with Hunter's Arsenal still on, so the save loads.
- Thunderwright's Kirkhammer's stats are unchanged.
- `tests/test_ng7_hammer.py` (new).

## NG+7 enemy attack limit

- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/enhancements.py`: `ng7_attack_cap` (`BB_PC_NG7_ATTACK_CAP`, default `off`), a row in Built-in enhancements with 2x, 1.5x and 1.2x. With NG+7 world on, NG+7 world's layer holds a gameparam, made from the layer below, where an NG+1 row's four attack rates are lowered so that rate times the cycle table's 1.2 is at most the limit. Enemy health, defence and echoes don't change. A player's mod with its own gameparam above the layer replaces the limit.
- `tests/test_ng7_attack_cap.py` (new).

## Spatial sound toggle

- `bloodborne_pc/launcher/bbport_launcher_win.py`, `bbport_launcher_backend.py`: Game & Saves, Audio: "Spatial sound", on by default. Off starts the game with `BB_AUDIO_SPATIAL=0`, which was only reachable through extra environment variables before.
- `tests/test_launcher_spatial_audio.py` (new).

## Thunder Chariot no longer whites out the screen

- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/thunderwright/weapon.py`: Thunder Chariot's flash covered up to a third of the screen near-white, with HDR on or off. The sky bolt and its warning, the launcher effect and two of every three strike effects are now unseen. Every strike still hits, and attack rows are unchanged. The NG+7 hammer shares these effects.

## Menu job guard

- `bloodborne_pc/src/menu_job_guard.c` (new, `menu-job-guard-test`): skips a queued menu job whose menu has already been freed, the new-game crash at image offset 0x1f591b0. It logs `Menu job guard: on`, and a line for each job it skips. The crash was not reproduced, so a clean run alone doesn't prove the fix.

## How it was tested

- The Python tests with `BB_TEST_GAME_DIR` (624 tests, 54 skipped) and `menu-job-guard-test`. `test_enhancements.RealGameTests.test_experimental_rematch_keeps_the_lamps_and_rebirth`, `test_mods.ModTests.test_run_uses_overlay_propagates_exit_and_cleans_view` and five `test_run_settings.RestartResolutionTests` fail the same way on 4.3.0.
- In play on a 4.3.1 build: the Stormforged Kirkhammer bought at +0 and upgraded to +10 has Radial, Triangle and Radial open and takes blood gems (read from the save). The menu job guard skipped 17 jobs in that run, with no fault and a clean exit; the new-game crash was not reproduced, so that doesn't show it is fixed.
- Seen in the launcher only: the Spatial sound toggle. Tests check that off reaches the game as `BB_AUDIO_SPATIAL=0`, 4.3.0's switch; the audio code is unchanged from 4.3.0, and the sound with the toggle off was not checked in play.
- Not yet tested in play: the attack limit (`tests/test_ng7_attack_cap.py` checks the params), the whiteout fix on this build, and the save conversion on a real save.
