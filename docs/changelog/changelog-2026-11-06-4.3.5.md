# 4.3.5 — NG+7 Sojin Chikage for a Bloodtinge build; Uncanny and Lost hammers (2026-11-06)

Built on 4.3.4.

## What changed

- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/ng7_chikage/` (new): Bloodsworn Sojin Chikage (2080000), Uncanny Bloodsworn Sojin (2100000) and Lost Bloodsworn Sojin (2110000). Each is its Sojin Chikage's rows with physical attack times 1.2 and Bloodtinge correction times 1.3, the NG+7 hammer's factors. Moves, abilities, model, gem slots, bleed and HP drain stay the Sojin Chikage's. Sold at +0 for 10,000 Blood Echoes, so gem slots open through upgrades. The Sojin Chikage's own rows are unchanged.
- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/thunderwright/` and `scripts/ng7_hammer/`: Uncanny and Lost versions of both hammers, built from the game's Uncanny and Lost Kirkhammers: Uncanny Thunderwright Hammer (2120000), Lost Thunderwright Hammer (2130000), Uncanny Stormforged Hammer (2140000) and Lost Stormforged Hammer (2150000). Stats match the normal version at every level. The gem slots at +10 are Radial, Triangle, Radial on the normal one, Radial, Waning, Radial on Uncanny and Radial, Triangle, Waning on Lost. They are sold at +0 under the existing switches, and the save converters turn each into its own Kirkhammer.
- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/enhancements.py`: `pc_ng7_chikage` (`BB_PC_NG7_CHIKAGE`, off by default), layer `ng7-chikage`. It brings `pc_sojin_chikage` with it, and is made on the NG+7 hammer's layer when that is on, so both weapons stay. The NG+7 attack limit is made on top of it.
- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/mods.py`: a mod's own gameparam or item text gets these weapons too.
- `bloodborne_pc/run.py`, `scripts/ng7_chikage/save_tool.py`: with the switch off, one in a save becomes its Chikage at the same level, so the save loads.
- `bloodborne_pc/launcher/bbport_launcher_win.py`, `gpu/shim/bbport_settings.h`, `bbport_settings.cpp`: the toggle, in the launcher and the in-game overlay.
- `bloodborne_pc/tests/test_ng7_chikage.py` (new).

## Checks

- Unit tests and the weapons built from the dump, alone and with the NG+7 hammer on.
- In play on the DLSS 5 test copy: the three Chikage versions show in the game. A save afterwards holds a Bloodsworn Sojin Chikage and an Uncanny Thunderwright Hammer at +0, and Uncanny and Lost Stormforged Hammers at +10, so buying and upgrading work. Three runs logged no fault and no rejected free, and each ended with a normal exit.
- **Not checked:** how much damage the weapons do in play, whether a Waning gem fits, and the save conversion on a real save. The ratings are calculated from the game's params.
