# 4.2.0 — NVIDIA DLSS: DLSS 4.5 and DLSS 5 measured at 4K, DLSS auto model fixed (2026-10-29)

Built on 4.1.0 (in the launcher only the DLSS model choices change). Results and recommendations: [DLSS 4.5 vs DLSS 5 at 4K](../dlss-4.2.0-comparison.md).

## Renderer

- **`dlss_model=auto` on RTX 40/50 now follows NVIDIA's defaults per quality mode** (`gpu/shim/bbport_nvidia_gpu.h` `BbDlssAutoPreset`): K for DLAA, Quality and Balanced, DLSS 4.5's M for Performance, L for Ultra Performance. It used to pick M for everything but Ultra Performance. On an RTX 4090 at 4K (same view, camera still) M cost more than K at every tier where K is NVIDIA's default: DLSS evaluate 3.89 against 1.19 ms at DLAA, 2.31 against 1.34 ms at Quality, 1.98 against 1.24 ms at Balanced, and up to 92 W more board power. On the benchmark route (depth of field on), before → after: DLAA 55.4 → 60.0 FPS (1% low 29.5 → 56.0, GPU busy 16.9 → 14.3 ms); Quality + frame generation 12.7 → 9.8 ms GPU busy; DLSS 5 Balanced with NR upscaling 54.3 → 60.0 FPS. RTX 20/30 keep K everywhere; a manually chosen model is unchanged. Test: `nvidia-gpu-test`.
- **`BB_FRAME_TIMES=<file>`** (new, `gpu/shim/bbport_frame_times.h`): one line per game flip (`g <ms>`), accepted present (`p <ms>`, generated frames included) and test mark (`m <text>`), so benchmarks can take 1% and 0.1% lows from single frames. Off without the variable.
- **`BB_PRESET_FILE`** takes an optional fourth number, the DLSS model (`BbSettings::DlssModel`), so a script can switch models live (`vk_temporal_upscaler.cpp`).

## Launcher and in-game menu

- DLSS model choices (`bbport_launcher_win.py` `DLSS_MODELS`, English and Russian; the row's help in `bbport_launcher_backend.py`; `bbport_overlay.cpp` `SettingsTab` and its hint) name each model by the modes Auto uses it for: "Auto: NVIDIA's pick per quality mode", "K: DLSS 4, for DLAA, Quality and Balanced", "M: DLSS 4.5 for Performance", "L: DLSS 4.5 for Ultra Performance". They used to say "M: DLSS 4.5, best on RTX 40 and 50". `features.md`'s Auto policy table follows.
- The NR upscaling row's help and the 4K warning (`dlss5_output_warning`) say what 4.2.0 measured at 4K on an RTX 4090: 60 FPS with DLSS Balanced or Performance, 60 → 120 with frame generation only at Ultra Performance, 55–57 FPS with Quality. They used to call Balanced "between them".

## Benchmark tools

- `dlss-bench-report.py` writes the tables, `dlss-bench-images.py` the crops, frames and clips.
- `nvidia-smi` (load, VRAM, power, clocks, temperature) and `typeperf` (CPU) while the game runs.
- The game window is kept on top for the whole session, and any window over it is logged to `overlaps.txt` before every step: `record` grabs the desktop (ddagrab), so a window in front ended up in two of the 4.2.0 clips. `dlss-bench-report.py` flags such runs, and runs that never made their Atlas travel.
- `BbOverlay::PollTests` (`bbport_overlay.cpp`, called by the presenter on frames the overlay skips): the map test hook (`BB_MAP_TEST_CONTROL`, `map-trace.json`) also runs when nothing is drawn. With the FPS counter off, the trace froze when the Atlas closed for a travel, and scripts never saw the travel arrive. Nothing changes without the variable.
- `scripts/build_dist.py`: the summary no longer fails when `--output-dir` is outside the repository.
