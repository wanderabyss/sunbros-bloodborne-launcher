# 4.2.1 — Unbuilt ray tracing options removed from the menus (2026-10-30)

Built on 4.2.0. No renderer changes.

## Launcher and in-game menu

- **Removed "Dynamic secondary light sources" and "Ray-traced water reflections"** from Visuals > Advanced settings > Ray tracing (`bbport_launcher_backend.py`: the rows, the section list and their search keywords) and from the in-game menu's ray tracing section (`bbport_overlay.cpp` `SettingsTab`). Both rows have been greyed out with "unavailable" since 3.3.1, and neither feature exists: there is no code that finds the game's torch and lantern lights, and the water reflection prototype in `vk_rt_effects.cpp` stays forced off because the renderer has no reflected scene colour or wet-surface mask. No work on either is planned.
- `rt_secondary_lights` and `rt_reflections` are still read from and written to `bbport.ini` (`bbport_settings.cpp`, `INI_FLAGS` in `bbport_launcher_win.py`), so settings files that have them keep loading. Test: `test_unimplemented_ray_tracing_controls_are_hidden_but_kept_in_the_ini`.

## Docs

- `bloodborne_pc/README.md` no longer says the port has ray-traced water reflections. Ray tracing is ambient occlusion and moon shadows.
- `features.md`: both keys are listed as kept in the INI with no launcher or in-game control since 4.2.1.

## Version

- 4.2.1 in `version.txt`, `launcher/version.txt`, `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock`, `bbport_launcher_win.py`, `HomePage.qml` and Release_Notes.md.
