# Changelog — October 29, 2026

## 4.0.0 — Major release

4.0.0 is the 3.4.1 follow-up renamed: it gathered enough new features (save backups, HDR brightness controls, DLSS 5 neural rendering through the community route, FSR 4.1.1 on Windows, FSR 1 present upscale) for a major version, and 3.4.1 was never published. Built on 3.4.0 (the DLSS roadmap).

- **HDR output levels.** `ToneMapBloodborneHDR` (`post_process.frag`) had fixed levels: SDR white at 120 nits, a shoulder from 600 to a 1000-nit ceiling and a 6.5× highlight expansion. They are now `bbport.ini` settings, applied at the next frame (`BbSettings` `HdrLevels`, `ResolveHdrLevels`):
  - `hdr_white_nits` (80–400, `BB_HDR_WHITE`), `hdr_peak_nits` (400–4000, `BB_HDR_PEAK`; the shoulder starts at 60 % of it, and the peak is never below white) and `hdr_highlight_boost` (0–1 of the 6.5× expansion, `BB_HDR_BOOST`).
  - 0 for white or peak takes the display's: Windows' SDR content brightness and the display's peak from SDL (`WindowSDL::ReadHdrLevels`).
  - Defaults are the old values (120 / 1000 / 1.00), saved only when changed, so nothing changes until a player picks one.
  - Launcher: three rows under HDR output in Visuals > Display & frame rate (HDR paper white, HDR peak brightness, HDR highlight boost).
  - Tests: `hdr-presentation-test` renders white 200 / peak 600 / no boost on the GPU (defaults still give 742.757 nits for white); `dlss-settings-test` covers parse, clamp, save and `ResolveHdrLevels`; `test_launcher_hdr_levels.py`. Not checked in game on an HDR display.

- **DLSS 5 neural rendering through the community route.** The port's own NeuralRender* bridge entry points (ABI 3) still report unsupported, so DLSS 5 runs through the player's own ReShade with dlss5-bridge and RenoDX's renodx-dlss5, plus their own `nvngx_dlssnr.dll` in `bin\`.
  - **Switch:** `bbport.ini` `dlss5` at the top of Visuals > Neural rendering (DLSS 5). On: `run.py` `dlss5_environment` keeps ReShade's Vulkan layer and sets `BB_DLSS5_ROUTE_A=1` when the three files are in `bin\`; otherwise the layer stays off and `BB_DLSS5_PROBLEM` names what is missing. Works for "Play Bloodborne.exe" as well as the launcher. Off: ReShade stays off. This replaces the "Apply to the RenoDX DLSS 5 add-on" toggle and the greyed DLSS 5 / Detail / Colour change rows on the Upscaling tab (`dlss5_detail` / `dlss5_tone` stay in `bbport.ini` for the built-in route).
  - **Look settings** (`nr_*` in settings.json): NR upscaling (first, as it decides whether 4K can hold 60), intensity, preset, style, local structure, local tone, skin structure, automatic mask, UI correction; intensity and structure go up to 2.00. Before each start with the switch on, PLAY writes `NeuralUplift=1` and these to `bin\ReShade.ini` `[RenoDX.DLSS5]` (`apply_renodx_nr`); *Add-on default* removes the key. "Reset to add-on defaults" clears them all.
  - **Status line:** checks `nvngx_dlssnr.dll`, `dlss5-bridge.addon64`, `renodx-dlss5.addon64` and (read only) that `C:\ProgramData\ReShade\ReShadeApps.ini` lists this folder's `bb-probe.exe`. The launcher never downloads, bundles or checks third-party files. A "DLSS 5 neural" button on the Visuals page opens the section.
  - **DLSS 5 ready by output size:** up to 1.5× the pixels of 1440p it keeps the player's output with DLSS Quality and ray tracing High (60/120 on an RTX 4090). Above that it sets 2560 × 1440 output, which the presenter upscales to the display, with Cinematic style, local structure 2.00 and NR upscaling off: 60 → 120 on an RTX 4090, sharper than NR upscaling at any 4K preset that held 60. Measured at 4K: NR upscaling held 60/120 with DLSS Performance (softer) and 55–57 FPS with Quality; native NR costs 14–16 ms a frame.
  - **Warnings:** `dlss5_output_warning` at 60 FPS on the DLSS 5, NR upscaling and output rows and in the start log; the game's too-slow log line, menu note and banner above 1440p name both ways out (2560 × 1440 output or NR upscaling). Presets treat Reflex On as a minimum, so On + Boost stays.
  - The package itself never ships the DLSS 5 files.
  - Player setup guide (files, where they go, bridge settings, troubleshooting): [docs/dlss5-setup.md](../dlss5-setup.md).

- **Presenting into a 0 × 0 swapchain.** A game window minimized mid-run kept its size in SDL3, so a failed present remade the swapchain at the surface's 0 × 0 extent and never again; every later frame ran an HDR scratch "resize", frame generation fell to 0 generated frames and Reflex latency rose from 57 to 72–77 ms. `Present()` now uses `ChoosePresentTarget` (`present_target.h`): with no area it skips presenting until the surface has one, then remakes the swapchain; the scratch is never made at 0 × 0. 4K, RTX 4090, Reflex Boost, FG 2×: 120 FPS shown, 57.4–57.8 ms, and 120 FPS on the first interval after a 15 s minimize. Tests: `present-target-test`.

- **Launcher hang on Exit to desktop.** Each game output line was its own GUI update that rebuilt the 350 KB log text; the ~2,300 lines the game prints when it quits froze the launcher for minutes (`AppHangB1`). `_read_output` now emits at most one batch per 100 ms (the run log still gets every line as it comes), and the status checks look at the whole batch. Longest GUI block with a replayed exit log: over 15 min before, 1.8 s after.

- **Smaller changes.** With `BB_DLSS_BACKEND=streamline`, DLSS frame generation names the Streamline backend as the reason it falls back to FSR 3.1 (Streamline 2.14.16 SR held 60/120 with FSR 3.1 FG at 4K; 54.5 ms latency against 50.8 ms on NGX with DLSS-G). Launcher card labels: the FSR 4.1.1 choice points to Visuals > Upscaling (all translations), FSR 3.1 frame generation reads "FSR 3.1 + 4.1.1" and DLSS frame generation "DLSS (RTX 40+)". `scripts/build_dist.py` writes one zip per build. Version 4.0.0 in `version.txt`, the launcher and `pyproject.toml`.

- **Presentation images that can't be allocated (S2).** `Presenter::RecreateFrame` now returns whether it made the image, and makes the new image before freeing the old one.
  - **Before:** on `ErrorInitializationFailed` it retried once after the GPU was idle, then asserted. A 15-minute run stopped there.
  - **Now:** after that retry, the policy in `frame_recreate.h` decides. A game frame being resized keeps its previous image (rendered at the old size), and the next frame tries again. The HUD-free copy is optional: frame generation goes on without it. The HDR scratch is optional too: that frame is composed straight into the swapchain without the HDR10 / SDR-on-HDR encode. A first image (nothing to keep) gets 5 more tries 100 ms apart.
  - **Back-off, never fatal with a fallback:** later failures in a streak retry after 1, 2, 4… frames (capped at 120) without waiting on the GPU; the first failure of a streak waits only for the calling thread's own scheduler (not `vkDeviceWaitIdle`) and retries once. Each caller (game frames, blank frames, HUD-free copy, HDR scratch) has its own policy, passed in explicitly. Fatal only for a first image with no fallback, after its retries.
  - **Frame generation:** paused for a frame that keeps its old size (sizes would alternate and rebuild FSR 3.1 / DLSS-G resources every frame).
  - **Logging:** the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 8th... failure in a row, each with the heap report; a 0 × 0 size is skipped with a warning.
  - **Testing:** `BB_TEST_FRAME_ALLOC_FAILS=N[@M]` makes N allocations fail after M presented game frames (plain `N`: from the start) with `ErrorInitializationFailed`. The policy is unit-tested in `presentation-telemetry-test`.
- **DLSS 5 Detail and Colour change (S3).** `bbport.ini` `dlss5_detail` (Structure Intensity, default 0.50) and `dlss5_tone` (Tone Intensity, default 0.00, decision D6: the game's own grading), 0–1, with `BB_DLSS5_DETAIL` / `BB_DLSS5_TONE` overrides.
  - `Dlss5::Inputs` reads them each frame, in place of the fixed 0.5 / 0.
  - The in-game menu API (`bbgpu_setting_get/set`) uses tenths, and `Save` writes each key once.
  - The launcher had two greyed rows after DLSS 5; they went with the community-route switch above, and the keys stay in `bbport.ini` for the built-in route.

- **Present upscale with FSR 1.** An output below the display (for example 2560 × 1440 on 3840 × 2160) is drawn into the display-sized presentation frame in `Presenter::PrepareFrame`.
  - **Before:** `post_process.frag`'s bilinear sampler stretched it. shadPS4's FSR 1 pass was there but only behind `BB_FSR1=1`. The present-queue blit is 1:1, except for one frame after a resize.
  - **Now:** FSR 1 EASU, then RCAS, by default whenever the picture is smaller than the frame (`BbSettings::PresentUsesEasu`). The HUD-less copy for frame generation gets the same treatment, and frames stay SDR RGBA8 with the HDR10 encode after them. EASU filters the square root of the linear (sRGB-view) samples, as FSR 1 expects a perceptual input.
  - **Controls:** `bbport.ini` `present_upscale = easu | linear` (default `easu`) and `present_rcas` (FSR stops 0–4, default 0 = no RCAS since the upscaler's sharpening already ran; `off` or a negative value also turn it off), with `BB_PRESENT_UPSCALE` / `BB_PRESENT_RCAS`. `BB_FSR1`, `BB_RCAS` and `BB_RCAS_ATTENUATION` are retired.
  - **Launcher:** Settings > Visuals > Display & frame rate, after Output resolution: "Upscale to the display" (FSR 1 EASU (recommended) / Bilinear (old)) and "Extra sharpening after upscale" (Off (default), Subtle 2.0, Light 1.0, Medium 0.5, Strong 0.25 stops). Hand-edited values load as the nearest row option (`bilinear` as Bilinear, other unknown upscales as EASU; RCAS clamped to 4 and snapped). Tested in `test_launcher_present_upscale.py` and `test_home_dashboard_qml.py`.
  - **Unchanged:** equal sizes (the same commands as before) and downscales (bilinear).
  - **Profiling:** `BB_GPU_PROFILE=1` labels `present: fsr1 easu`, `present: fsr1 rcas` and `present: compose`, with a `UI-free` variant for the HUD-less copy.
  - **Fixes in the FSR pass:** barriers before its images are reused (their last reader is a fragment shader), and the EASU-only output image is moved to `General` before it is written.
  - **Tests:** `output-settings-test`. In game: the DLSS 5 A/B is pending a user run.

- **DLSS texture mip bias −1 by default.** `BbSettings::DlssMipOffsetDefault` = −1, added to `log2(render / output)` in `TemporalUpscaler::SceneMipBias` (NVIDIA's DLSS guide). `Save` writes `dlss_mip_offset` only when it differs from −1, and `Load` resets it, so files without the key follow the default and an explicit value (including 0) is kept. Evidence: mip bias A/B. Tests: `dlss-settings-test`.

- **Custom output sizes.** `patches.py output_size` and `bbport_settings.cpp` `Set("output_res")` rejected odd sides, so `output_res=3682x2071` fell back to 1920 × 1080 (scene patch, upscaler output, `WindowedSize`); both now round down to even and accept any size from 256 × 144 to 7680 × 4320 (a listed size takes its index). `patches.py --print-scaled` takes `--output-res` and `run.py` / `run.sh` pass `BB_OUTPUT_RES`, which it ignored, and a user's `BB_OUTPUT_RES` survives in-game restarts (`BB_USER_OUTPUT_RES`). Launcher list: 2880 × 1620, 3200 × 1800. Tests: `test_patches.py`, `test_run_settings.py`, `test_launcher_output.py`, `output-settings-test`; in game 3200 × 1800 on 4K, DLSS Quality 2136 × 1200 → 3200 × 1800, `Present upscale: 3200x1800 -> 3682x2071 with FSR 1 EASU`, 60/120 FPS, 0 failed presents.

- **Catmull-Rom magnification for movie-only bitmaps above 1080p.** Startup logos and the title are Scaleform bitmaps drawn straight into the 4K UI image (native composition), already linear since 3.3.1 (`SmoothMenuBitmap`); captures showed no point sampling, just low-resolution artwork (Japan Studio logo ~4x at 4K) whose bilinear magnification left 4–5 px edge ramps and kinked curves. Fragment shaders paired with a `UiComposition::MovieShader` vertex shader now compile with `HwFragmentRuntimeInfo::bitmap_cubic` (`vk_pipeline_cache.cpp`); their 2D implicit-LOD samples (`emit_spirv_image.cpp` `BitmapCubic`) keep the game's bilinear sample and, behind a uniform branch on `PushData::BitmapCubicFlag` (top bit of `motion_param`), replace it where LOD < 0 with a 9-tap bilinear Catmull-Rom of mip 0 clamped to [0, 1]. `Rasterizer::ApplyBitmapCubic` sets the flag when `UiComposition::CubicMenuBitmap` (movie-only bitmap draw, target scale > 1, normalized sampler, no `BB_UI_BITMAP_FILTER=linear`). `ShaderBinaryVersion` 9. Tests: `ui-composition-test`.

- **Save backups in the launcher.** New `launcher/bbport_save_backups.py` (pure functions) and a Save backups section in Settings > Game & Saves (`SettingsWorkspace.qml`).
  - **Backups:** `<saves folder>\save-backups\<YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS>[ - label].zip` holds `savedata/...` and `manifest.json` (time, kind `manual` / `auto` / `safety`, label, character summary from `bbport_character`, file count, bytes). Written as `.<name>.part` and renamed.
  - **Restore:** checks the zip (CRC, manifest), backs up the current saves (`before restore`, kind `safety`), unpacks beside `savedata`, checks file count and size, swaps the folders, checks again. On any failure the previous folder is moved back, or the safety backup is unpacked if it cannot be. Entries outside `savedata/` are refused.
  - **Automatic:** `backup_after_play` (default on) backs up when a game that got as far as running exits; `_receive_exit` only starts the worker thread, so the exit path stays fast. The newest 10 automatic backups are kept; manual and safety backups are never pruned.
  - **Guards:** backup and restore are refused while the game runs or a co-op start is busy (the buttons are disabled with a notice), and PLAY waits for a running backup. Delete and Restore confirm in the row (no dialog); Open backups folder.
  - **Tests:** `test_save_backups.py` (backup, labels, atomic write, list, prune, delete, restore, rollback on a failed check and on a locked folder, damaged and unsafe zips) and `test_save_backups_qml.py` (worker, refusal while running, exit hook, the section's buttons).

- **FSR 4.1.1 on Windows through fsr4vk (experimental).** `IsFsr411Supported` (the replay in `fsr411/`) needs `VK_VALVE_shader_mixed_float_dot_product`, which no Windows driver exposes, so `upscaler=fsr411` never ran on Windows. New `vk_fsr4vk.cpp` / `.h`, ported from 0xCydral/bloodborne_pc (same code lineage), load fsr4vk's FFX provider `amd_fidelityfx_upscaler_vk.dll` at run time from `BB_FSR4VK_DIR` or `fsr4vk` beside `bb-probe.exe`; the port does not link or ship it.
  - **Device:** when the DLL is present (`Fsr4Vk::FilesPresent`, `BB_FSR4VK=0` opts out) and the GPU has them, `Instance::CreateDevice` enables `VK_EXT_mutable_descriptor_type`, `VK_EXT_descriptor_buffer`, `runtimeDescriptorArray`, `descriptorBindingVariableDescriptorCount` and `shaderStorageImageReadWithoutFormat`. New `IsFsr4VkSupported`, `IsFsr411Available` (replay or fsr4vk) and `IsRobustBufferAccessEnabled`. The temporal upscaler's support checks use `IsFsr411Available`.
  - **Dispatch:** `Fsr4Upscaler::Impl::Record411` uses fsr4vk when the replay can't run. Inputs are RGBA16F color, D32S8 depth (depth aspect, R32 with the depth and stencil usage hints), RG16F motion in render pixels; eight frames in flight, matching the provider's slot ring; presets map to its forced presets (0, 1, 2, 3, 5).
  - **NVIDIA limit:** on an RTX 4090 the provider's 2160 tier (any output above 1920 × 1080) loses the device on its first dispatch (`ReadInvalid` device fault) at 2560 × 1440 and 3840 × 2160, with or without frame generation; the 1080 tier runs. `CreateContextNow` refuses those outputs on vendor 0x10DE unless `BB_FSR4VK_ANY_SIZE=1`, and the game falls back to FSR 3.1 with the reason in the log and menu.
  - **Launcher:** `launcher/bbport_fsr4vk.py` downloads the pinned zip (v0.4.3 plus the AMD driver patch that drops the variable descriptor count flag from three heap bindings, fsr4vk issue #1), checks the zip's and each file's SHA-256, and writes `SOURCE.txt`; `python launcher/bbport_fsr4vk.py --check` verifies a folder. Settings > Visuals > Upscaling gains "Download FSR 4.1.1" with a status line (`downloadFsr4Vk`, `fsr4vkStatus`), and the upscaler label reads "FSR 4.1.1 (experimental, download in Graphics)".
  - **In game (RTX 4090):** 1280 × 720 → 1920 × 1080 Quality, `Upscaler: FSR 4.1.1 through fsr4vk, FSR 4.1.1 VK INT8, quality model, output 1920x1080`, 60 FPS, clean picture; with DLSS-G 2× and with FSR 3.1 frame generation 2×, 60 → 120 FPS, no fallback. At 4K the guard selects FSR 3.1 and DLSS-G keeps 120 FPS. Results.
