# 4.4.2 — Fix: a freeze while loading after lamp travel (2026-11-07)

Built on 4.4.1.

## Why

With DLSS 5 and frame generation at 3840 x 2160 output, the game could freeze while loading after a few lamp travels. Every thread waited and the game used no CPU. 4.3.2 listed this as "Lamp travel at 4K (not fixed)".

The game keeps lock owners in 40 bits, so the port gives guest thread and lock handles addresses below 1 TiB, from a range at 60 to 64 GiB. That range was not reserved. About a minute after start, memory the port does not allocate (large reservations and 32 MiB blocks, likely from the graphics driver or the DLSS 5 add-ons) had taken it and nearly all of the space below it. Once the current handle block ran out, which took a few travels, new handles came from the host heap above 1 TiB, and the log said so: `guest handle ... is above 1 TiB; the game's 40-bit lock owners cannot hold it`. A thread with such a handle does not see itself as a lock's owner and waits for good. That is the loading freeze `runtime_memory.c` already describes.

## What changed

- `bloodborne_pc/src/runtime_memory.c`: `runtime_memory_reserve` reserves the handle range at start, before the graphics driver loads, and `low_map_from` commits handle blocks inside it. If something already uses part of the range, the log says so and handles take what is free there, as before.
- `bloodborne_pc/src/runtime_thread.c`: Windows places guest thread stacks itself. The log now says once if a guest thread's stack is above 1 TiB.
- `bloodborne_pc/tests/test_low_handles.c`: the range is reserved after start, nothing else can be placed in it, and handles and a large block come from it.

## DLSS model M in the Remastered and DLSS 5 ready presets

- `bloodborne_pc/launcher/bbport_launcher_win.py`: both presets set `dlss_model=M` instead of `auto`, and their descriptions say so. On RTX 40 and 50 cards Auto picks K at Quality, NVIDIA's default. The 4.2.0 comparison found M 0.7 to 2.7 ms slower than K at Quality at 4K and no sharper in still images. The other presets and the default stay on Auto.
- `bloodborne_pc/tests/test_graphics_presets.py`: both presets choose M.

## Checks

All on an RTX 4090 at 3840 x 2160 output with DLSS 5, frame generation 2x at 60 FPS, DLSS Quality with model M and RT Ultra, through all 45 lamps but one (lamp 18 starts Cainhurst's arrival cutscene on the test save). The DLC lamps were included.

| Build | Runs | Froze | Lamp travels | "above 1 TiB" in the log |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.4.1 | 3 | 2, after 5 travels each | 51 | 3 of 3 runs |
| 4.4.2 | 6 | 0 | 246 | 0 of 6 runs |

- 3 more 4.4.2 runs through the same lamps with the "DLSS 5 ready" preset (2560 x 1440 output scaled to the 4K screen, DLSS Quality, RT High, frame generation 2x at 60 FPS) didn't freeze either, and none logged a handle above 1 TiB. They held a median of 60 game FPS (10th percentile 57.6 to 58.4), against 38 to 41 at 4K output above.
- Native HDR was on in half the runs and off in the other half on both builds. It made no difference: 4.4.1 froze with it on and with it off.
- One 4.4.1 run passed the whole route with a handle above 1 TiB, so that handle doesn't freeze every time.
- No thread stack was above 1 TiB in the 4.4.2 runs.
- A frozen 4.4.1 game's thread list and minidump were kept. Which lock it was waiting on wasn't read from them.

## Limits

- The Oct 29 and Nov 5 heap-free faults after lamp travel had no DLSS 5, and this change is not known to fix them.
- With DLSS 4.5, 4 runs of 4.4.2 through the same lamps at 4K (DLSS Quality and DLAA, RT Ultra, frame generation 2x at 60 FPS, Native HDR on in 2) neither froze nor faulted. The 4.3.2 DLSS 4.5 fault (1 in 10, without frame generation) wasn't repeated in that exact setup.
- The frame rates above were measured with model K, which Auto picked at Quality. Remastered with M was then run twice through the same lamps at 4K: a median of 60 game FPS (10th percentile 59.0) and about 120 shown with frame generation, against 59.2 to 59.4 with K. The 2 runs were clean, so 4.4.2 had 15 runs with no freeze. "DLSS 5 ready" with M wasn't run through the lamps.
- Only the RTX 4090 was checked. Other cards and drivers may place their memory elsewhere.
