# 4.2.16 — Sojin Chikage balance: Bankai is earned, the twin blades match the Chikage (2026-11-01)

Built on 4.2.15.

## Why

Play tests of 4.2.15's Sojin Chikage found it too strong and without risk: Bankai could kill a full-HP enemy in one move, blood loss came about every second hit, and the heal on hit outweighed the drain. A review against the game's own numbers then found what the 65 % damage scale had not covered: the twin blades staggered enemies 1.5x as fast as the Chikage's transformed form and did 27 % more damage per stamina point, the R2 R2 finisher and L2 charged HP up to 15 s after the player stopped building Blood Rhythm, and Bankai's 30 s were better spent in katana form, where it had no drain.

## What changed

All in `bloodborne_pc/scripts/sojin/` (`weapon.py`, `abilities.py`, `ability_events.py`). Normal mode (the Chikage's katana) is unchanged.

**Twin blades**
- Stagger (poise) and stamina damage take the same 65 % as the damage, and stamina costs are 1.25x the Blade of Mercy's. The R1 chain now matches the Chikage's transformed one: 165 against 167 motion value a second, 165 against 167 stagger a second, 4.0 damage per stamina point for both.
- Drain 0.4 → 0.6 % HP a second while drawn (the Chikage: 0.85 %).
- Bleed 20 → 15 a hit. The Sojin weapons' blood loss deals 6 % of the enemy's max HP + 50; the Chikage's own (10 % + 100) is unchanged. The transform attack back to the katana builds 20 (was the Chikage's 30).
- Rows no animation uses follow the same rules (katana rows from the Chikage, twin rows scaled), so a moveset change cannot hit at the Blade of Mercy's full values.

**Blood Rhythm:** damage only (+4 % a stack, up to five; no +1 bleed a stack); the stacks drop 3 s after the last hit (was 5), so the full +20 % rewards staying on the attack.

**Bankai**
- Needs full Blood Rhythm: the fifth stack keeps a "ready" effect (the red icon) on, and every cost and effect of Bankai is valid only while it lasts. It lasts 4 s after full Rhythm ends, long enough to go back to the katana and cast Awakening from there. Without full Rhythm the three moves below are the plain moves and cost nothing.
- **Awakening** (R1, then L1 mid-swing from the katana): about 20 % of current HP; for 30 s, +10 % damage, +5 bleed and a heal of 0.2 % max HP on every hit, all only with the blood blades out (one-second effects the 30 s timer gives again every second, as a trick-mode condition on the 30 s effects themselves was rejected in game). No extra drain. The shockwave deals 40 % of the attack rating and no blood hit.
- **Crimson Moon** (R2 R2 in blood form): about 10 % of current HP for 1.2 s of x1.6 damage and +15 bleed over the finisher and its Blood Crescent.
- **Blood Ward** (L2 in blood form): about 10 % of current HP for 20 s of 20 % less damage taken (the defence-up icon).
- Costs come in twenty 1 % steps (one at each 5 % of HP): about 20 % (or 10 %) of current HP, never lethal, free only below 5 % HP (was below 10 %).
- Costs cannot be regained by rally (`regainGaugeDamage` 0, as the Chikage's own drain). They were copied from the Bloodletter's transform self-damage, which can be: the cost lands 2–3 frames before the move's own hit, and that hit won it all back (a recorded play test showed a Blood Ward's 7 % back within 0.1 s), so every Bankai that connected was free.
- Bankai's effects start once: their starters ticked six times in 0.5 s, which gave the heal and bleed several times over.

## How it was tested

- Made from the 1.09 dump and checked row by row against its sources (`weapon.verify`), the ability and TAE self-tests (`python abilities.py`, `python ability_events.py`) and `tests/test_sojin.py` with `BB_TEST_GAME_DIR`.
- Measured from the game files: combo timing from each animation's first combo window, damage, stagger and stamina per second for the Chikage, the Blade of Mercy and both Sojin modes.
- Played on the 4.2.15 launcher with these scripts (before the rally fix): Bankai and blood loss checked in play. The session was recorded and its HUD read frame by frame: Blood Ward lasts 20 s, Blood Rhythm drops 3 s after the last hit, Bankai stays ready 4 s, Awakening's power and heal last 30 s.
- The rally fix is checked offline only (the cost effects carry `regainGaugeDamage` 0); not yet in play.
- Not yet tested: co-op, plunge attacks.
