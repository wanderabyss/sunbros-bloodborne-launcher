# 4.2.18 — Thunderwright's Kirkhammer balance: split damage, Arcane 25; damage log (2026-11-01)

Built on 4.2.17.

## Why

Read from the 4.2.17 gameparam, Thunderwright's Kirkhammer +10 is the Kirkhammer's whole physical (210, Strength 100) plus 180 bolt (Arcane 100), with only Arcane 12 needed. Nearly any Strength build could carry it at about 1.7 times the Kirkhammer's attack rating, while the Sojin Chikage was balanced in 4.2.16 to equal the Chikage. It now splits its damage the way the game's own weapons with a built-in element do (Ludwig's Holy Blade: 200 + 50), needs Arcane 25 so it is an Arcane build's weapon, and Static Draw's free charge is short.

## What changed

- `bloodborne_pc/scripts/thunderwright/weapon.py`: `ARCANE_NEEDED` 12 -> 25 (`properFaith` on all eleven levels; `verify()` checks the new value). Below the requirement the game's own unmet-requirement penalty cuts the weapon's attack.
- Physical: `attackBasePhysics` is `PHYSICAL_SCALE` (150 / 210) of the Kirkhammer's at each level, rounded half up: 75 at +0, 150 at +10. Before, the field was copied unchanged.
- Bolt: `BOLT_BASE, BOLT_STEP` 90, 9 -> 55, 5.5, rounded half up (`bolt(level)`): 55 at +0, 110 at +10.
- Static Draw: its launcher now gives `DRAW_CHARGE` (2060004), a second copy of the charged Tonitrus's 6032 lasting `DRAW_CHARGE_TIME` 4 s, in place of Thunder Chariot's 10 s `STORED_CHARGE`, which is unchanged.
- `verify()` checks the physical, the bolt and the new effect.
- Storm look: `STORM_EFFECT` (2060005), a copy of the Tonitrus's 6033 with only `validCond_PCWeponState` 2 (trick mode) and `stateInfo` 61 (SpEffectVfxParam 61: sfx 502 at dummy 206, Bolt Paper 2220's own look; 151 "bolt enhancement" was ruled out, its sfx 22724 is in none of 1.09's effect banks), in the weapon's free `residentSpEffectId2`. It shows over the hammer effect's charged crackle (stateInfo 30, sfx 7210).
- Transform flash: bullet 2060080, Tiny Tonitrus's launcher 410 with no attack, flight, follow-up or effect, `life` 0.05 s and the strikes' hit effect (bullet 428's `sfxId_Hit`, 203007), fired from the hammer head (dummy 12100) by behaviour judge 991 on the four transform attacks (33300/33310 into the hammer, 43300/43310 Static Draw) at their impact frame. `verify()` checks both.
- Sky lightning (`scripts/thunderwright/sky.py`, new): Thunder Chariot (42630, 42660) also fires judge 993, bullets 2060090-2060092, copies of the Fishing Hamlet fishman priests' spell 405111 (positioning dummy, rises ~2 m), 405112 (warning, sfx 640505) and 405113 (falling bolt, sfx 640506, hit 640507) with `atkId_Bullet` -1 and the chain ended at the bolt. Those effects are only in `sfx/frpg_sfxbnd_m36` (the Fishing Hamlet's bank), so `sky.common_bank()` adds them to `frpg_sfxbnd_commoneffects`: the closure of the three roots over m36-only effects, models and textures (an effect names others and its models by number; a model names its textures), 24 files and +0.76 MB in 1.09. Each keeps m36's name, data and flags and takes the next free id of its kind. `sky.verify()` checks the common bank's own files unchanged and the added ones byte-identical to m36's. A failure there leaves the bank out; the weapon is still made.
- Unchanged: Strength and Arcane scaling, the hammer's 76 % physical, the sword's half-bolt hits, the lightning lines' bolt (50 / 80 / 150), Thunder Chariot's cost and charge.
- The enhancement generator's identity hashes `scripts/thunderwright/*.py`, so Hunter's Arsenal's layer is remade at the next start with no `VERSION` bump.
- `bloodborne_pc/README.md`: the new numbers. `tests/test_thunderwright.py`: Arcane 25, the split and the 4 s Static Draw charge.

## How it was tested

- `tests/test_thunderwright.py` with `BB_TEST_GAME_DIR` (the weapon made from the 1.09 dump and verified).
- `tests/test_sojin.py` (Hunter's Arsenal builds the Sojin Chikage first) and `tests/test_version_source.py`.
- The 4.2.18 package's built gameparam read back: the new physical, bolt and Arcane on 2060000-2061000.
- Not yet tested in play, including whether the flash bullet shows its hit effect when it ends without hitting anything, as the strikes do, and whether the sky bolt shows in full outside the Fishing Hamlet (the closure is read from the effects' numbers, not from a format spec). A mod that brings its own common effect bank would replace Hunter's Arsenal's, leaving the sky bolt unseen outside the Fishing Hamlet.

## Damage log

### Why

Balancing Thunderwright's Kirkhammer needed real damage numbers, and the game's log had none: it records settings and performance only.

### What changed

- `bloodborne_pc/src/damage_log.c` (new): every HP change of the player and the characters they fight, as `Damage log` lines in `last_run.log`. A character's HP is in its data module `[[ChrIns + 0x3b0] + 0x20]` (HP +0xf8, max +0xfc, owner ChrIns +0x08; from bbsc-0.0.2's `GetBbscLocalPlayerHp`).
- How it finds hits: it reads WorldChrMan's character list twice a second (count at `+0x1068`, 0x38-byte entries from the pointer at `+0x1070`, ChrIns at `+0`; found by reading a running game's memory, 89 entries all valid characters) and polls every listed character's HP each frame. Before any change is a line, the owner chain is checked again and HP must make sense (0 <= HP <= max <= 10,000,000): in play, memory freed at an area change once read as HP changes of a billion. Besides that, `bb_hook_mid` on fourteen HP writes discovers modules too: the thirteen that share the HP bar's redraw tail (found by scanning the 1.09 eboot for `mov [reg + 0xf8], r32` followed by it), plus the max-HP setup at `0x1a567ab`, which every character runs as it loads. The main-thread tick keeps those modules in a table (owner chain checked when first seen and every second) and reads each one's HP every frame; any change is a line, whatever code wrote it. Hooked writes are logged as written. This design came from play: with only the thirteen hooked writes, only the death write (`0x18c4a2b`) ever fired, so ordinary hits change HP somewhere else.
- The player is WorldChrMan (`0x553e878`) + 0x60 and is always watched; enemies are numbered as they are first hurt, with their max HP; enemy HP rising (reset, load) is not logged; the player's heals are summed until a second passes without one. Diagnostics: a one-time "main-thread tick running" line, a ten-second summary (writes per site, characters tracked, drops and why), the first few rejected writes in full.
- Hunter's Atlas lamp travel (`runtime_atlas_state.c`) hooks the frame-time manager's entry (`0x2034770`) before the engine hooks, so `bb_main_frame_install` was refused, silently, and no `bb_on_main_frame` tick ran while travel was on (the damage log, mouse_camera's flick check, jump_decay, occlusion stats). `runtime_atlas_on_frame()` now runs the engine's ticks (`bb_main_frame_run`) from Atlas's bridge, and `engine_features.c` logs which way the tick runs or why it could not.
- On only with `BB_DAMAGE_LOG=1`; `run.py` sets it from `bbport.ini` `damage_log=1`. `gpu/CMakeLists.txt` builds it into bb-probe.
- `tests/test_damage_log.c` (new, `damage-log-test`): a real site's bytes in a small executable image, hooked and called: the write still happens, enemy damage and kills are logged, a write to memory that is not a character's module is dropped, the player's damage and summed heals are logged, and a character seen through an unchanged write and then hit by unhooked code is caught by the frame's poll.

### How it was tested

- `damage-log-test`, `engine-hooks-test`.
- In game (4.2.18 builds of 2026-11-01): all thirteen redraw-tail sites hook; the Atlas fix made the tick run (kills logged through `0x18c4a2b`); with the hooks and the max-HP setup alone, the player's damage and heals were logged correctly but enemies that streamed in after the first load were never tracked, which the character list fixes; enemy hits through the list not yet seen in play.
