# 4.2.17 — Hunter's Arsenal: Thunderwright's Kirkhammer joins the Sojin Chikage (2026-11-01)

Built on 4.2.16.

## Why

A second new weapon, without a blood theme: a Kirkhammer charged with bolt whose hammer throws lightning. It shares files with the Sojin Chikage (the gameparam, `c0000.anibnd` and the item texts), and mods replace whole files, so the two cannot be separate layers. They become one switch, **Hunter's Arsenal**, that makes both weapons in one layer. It keeps the Sojin Chikage's key (`pc_sojin_chikage`, `BB_PC_SOJIN`), so a player who had the Sojin Chikage on has both after updating and no save is converted.

## What changed

**Thunderwright's Kirkhammer** (`bloodborne_pc/scripts/thunderwright/weapon.py`, new)
- Eleven weapons, 2060000 + 100 per level, copied from the Kirkhammer's rows of the same level (model, motion category 48, gem slots, Strength 16 / Skill 10 requirement). Behaviour variation 2060 copies the Kirkhammer's 800.
- Bolt 90 + 9 a level; Strength and Arcane scaling (Arcane = the Kirkhammer's Strength correction, Skill 0); needs Arcane 12; `isEnhance` 0, so no papers (as every game weapon with a built-in element).
- Sword hits: the Kirkhammer's physical plus half of each motion value as bolt. Hammer hits: 76 % of the physical plus the full bolt. The hammer carries the charged Tonitrus's look (stateInfo 30) and bolt hits (special attribute 6), in trick mode only.
- Lightning: lines of Tiny Tonitrus's strikes (bullets 410-428, each line its own copies, wider hit radii) fired from the hammer head by type-318 events added to `a105.tae`, the Kirkhammer's moveset. The events name behaviour judges (931, 951, 961, 971, 981) that only variation 2060 has, so a plain Kirkhammer is unaffected. Small (1 × 6 strikes) on R1-type hits and both transform attacks, medium (1 × 12) on R2-type hits and a part charge, high (3 × 18, Thunder Chariot) on the charged R2.
- The charged R2 also fires an unseen bullet whose effect on the wielder takes one Quicksilver Bullet (`addBulletNum` -1, as the game's own effect that takes the player's bullets) and then becomes the charged Tonitrus's buff (bolt x1.7 +40) for 10 s. The transform back to the sword gives the same buff without the cost.
- Why category 48 and not a new one: the action script (`c0000.hks`) switches category-specific moves, the charged R2 among them, on lists of motion categories; a new category is on none of them, and its charged R2 never starts.
- `make()` checks every new row and the moveset against their sources (`verify()`): original rows and archive files byte-identical, only planned fields changed.

**Hunter's Arsenal** (`enhancements.py`, `mods.py`, `run.py`, launcher, overlay)
- The `pc_sojin_chikage` switch's layer is now `hunters-arsenal`: the Sojin Chikage's files, then Thunderwright's made on top of them. Either weapon is left out alone if the dump does not allow it. Enhancements version 7.
- `mods.py`: a mod above the layer with its own gameparam or item text (ShopEdit) gets whichever of the two weapons it lacks added to its copy; the shop rows follow the talk script on top (ShopEdit's own range).
- `run.py`: with the switch off and no mod providing them, Sojin Chikage weapons in a save become Chikages (as before) and Thunderwright's Kirkhammers become the Kirkhammer of the same level (`scripts/thunderwright/save_tool.py`, the Sojin Chikage's converter on this weapon's ids), each checked on its own. Backups go beside the save folder.
- Launcher Mods page and the in-game overlay call the switch Hunter's Arsenal.

## How it was tested

- `tests/test_thunderwright.py` (new), `tests/test_sojin.py` and `tests/test_enhancements.py` with `BB_TEST_GAME_DIR`: the weapon's files made from the 1.09 dump and verified, the lightning never added twice, Hunter's Arsenal's layer holding both weapons and the Sojin Chikage's model. `test_experimental_rematch_keeps_the_lamps_and_rebirth` fails as on 4.2.16 (it expects the PC menu on by default, which it no longer is).
- Offline with the real files: the layer made and reused, ShopEdit's gameparam merged with both weapons (Thunderwright's shop row in ShopEdit's range), and a copy of a save holding Thunderwright's Kirkhammer +2 converted to Kirkhammer +2 and read back.
- Played on stand-alone mod builds made by the same builder: the weapon in the shop, both forms, the charged R2 slam and the lightning lines. The final sizes, the Quicksilver Bullet cost, the Arcane requirement, the paper block and the switch itself are checked offline only.
- Not yet tested: co-op.
