# 4.2.7 — Save settings and a tidier Visuals page; Download BBSC back on the Co-op page (2026-10-30)

Built on 4.2.6 (the co-op server ships again). Launcher only; no game or renderer changes.

## Why

Every Visuals change was already saved as it was made, but only as "the last state": trying another preset replaced it, with no way back to a setup you liked.

## What changed

- `bbport_launcher_win.py`:
  - `MY_SETTINGS_INI` and `MY_SETTINGS_APP`: the keys the Visuals page sets (the presets' keys plus output, upscaler, DLSS model, ray tracing tiers, frame generation, sharpening, HDR levels, the display upscale, DLSS 5 and the NR look rows).
  - `my_settings_snapshot`, `saved_my_settings`, `my_settings_match` and `apply_my_settings`. Keys a later version dropped are skipped on load.
  - `graphics_preset_of` checks the named presets first, then `'mine'` (the saved set, shown as the Custom pill), then `'custom'`: settings saved on Ultra start on Ultra.
  - `GRAPHICS_PRESET_INFO['mine']` is labelled "Custom".
  - `APP_DEFAULTS`: `my_settings`.
- `bbport_launcher_backend.py`:
  - `saveMySettings` and `hasMySettings`.
  - `graphicsPresets` adds `'mine'` once saved, and `applyGraphicsPreset('mine')` loads the saved set.
  - At construction the saved set, when there is one, is applied after the saved `ini_settings` and written to `bbport.ini`.
  - `graphicsPreset` notifies on its own signal, `presetStateChanged`, sent by `changed`, `presetApplied` and every `setValue`. One field change emitted neither, so the preset row kept the old preset lit.
  - `presetInfo(name)`: a tuned preset's description and its ★ suggestion line, for its "?".
- `VisualsStudio.qml`:
  - Row 1: Low, Balanced, High, Ultra and one Custom pill (`studioPreset_custom`) for `'custom'` and `'mine'`. Row 2: `RTX 4090 | RTX 4080` (`tunedCard_4090`; `tunedCard_4080` disabled) then Remastered DLSS 4 and DLSS 5 (`PresetButton`), each with a "?" (`presetInfo_<name>`, hover ToolTip).
  - The description paragraph (`presetDescription`) only for a row-1 preset.
  - Right column (`visualsShortcuts`, 340 px when the window is wide enough): Save settings (`saveMySettings`), HDR output, HD textures, DLSS 5 neural and Advanced settings.
- Co-op: the 4.1.0 launcher rewrite (`a5c3e0c`, 2026-10-27) dropped the only button that called `LauncherBackend.coopSetup`, while `coopStatus` still said "Set up co-op downloads BBSC…". New `coopInstalled` and `coopVersion` properties, and a "Download BBSC 0.0.2" / "… again" button in `CoopSetupPanel.qml`. `coopPlay` and `coopStartHosting` still download missing packages (`_coop_ensure_packages`).
- `CoopSetupPanel.qml`: two steps (Account, Connection). Connection ends with Play co-op (`coopGuidedPlay`) and Return home; the Launch step (a summary of the same choices, `coopLaunchSummary`) and "Review launch" (`coopConnectionNext`) are gone. On the Co-op page (`CoopWorkspace.qml`, `actionsBeside: true`) Download BBSC (`coopSideDownload`, Account step), Play co-op (`coopSidePlay`) and Return home sit in the side column's Before you play card; Settings > Co-op keeps them in the panel. The panel's row is Show connection details and < Account. Tests: `tests/test_coop_settings_qml.py`, `tests/test_depth_workspaces_qml.py`.
- Co-op connection details: `bbport_coop.connection_checks` (BBSC, firewall rule, network, the host's TCP ports `GUEST_PORTS` or the public server's, or hosting state) and `connection_events` (the newest `bbsc-timeline-*.txt`, kinds `RELAY`, `JOIN`, `SESSION`). Backend `checkCoopConnection` (a thread; `coopChecks`, `coopChecking`) and `refreshCoopEvents` (`coopEvents`, every 3 s while the details are open). `CoopSetupPanel.qml`: "Show connection details" on the Connection step. **Open ports (admin)** (`coopOpenPorts`, backend `openCoopPorts`) runs `coop.run_setup` or `run_public_setup` (one administrator prompt) and checks again; `coopFirewallMissing` highlights it. Tests: `tests/test_coop.py`, `tests/test_depth_workspaces_qml.py`.
- `bloodborne_pc/patches/Bloodborne_exit_game.xml` (Nerkuu's Exit Game 1.01, title menu) is no longer in the repository or the package; `bloodborne_pc/README.md`'s credit says how to add it. `patches/Bloodborne.xml` and `redzone.json` stay.
- `bbport_coop.download('client')` replaced BBSC's folder whole, taking `bbsc/logs`, `bbsc/save-backups` (BBSC's before-bell save backups) and `bbsc/captures` with it. They are now moved into the new folder (`CLIENT_KEEP`), as the server package already kept its database and logs. Test: `tests/test_coop.py`.
- `README.md`: the launcher media are 4.2.7's. A landscape tour (`docs/media/launcher-4.2.7-tour.mp4`, 1460 × 940, 30 FPS; `docs/images/launcher-4.2.7-tour.webp` full width) replaces the 4.1.0 portrait tour, which left the column beside it blank. Ten screenshots (`docs/images/launcher-4.2.7-*.png`) replace the 4.1.0 ones, rendered from the live QML with an example hunter, example trophies and an example co-op invite (nothing from the recording PC's settings, saves or BBSC logs).
- Tests:
  - `tests/test_depth_workspaces_qml.py`: the Co-op page offers the download, and its label once installed.
  - `tests/test_graphics_presets.py`: save, match, load, and keys that are dropped or unknown.
  - `tests/test_depth_workspaces_qml.py`: Save settings on a preset and on a custom mix, the Custom pill, start on Ultra after saving on Ultra, the tuned row and its "?" marks, the right column.
  - `tests/test_workspace_navigation_qml.py`: `click` scrolls an item below the window into view first.
