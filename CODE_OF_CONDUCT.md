# Code of conduct

This project is made by people who play and fix a game in their spare time. Treat everyone here with respect, in issues, pull requests and Discussions.

## Expected

- Be patient and assume good faith, especially with people new to the project or to modding.
- Keep criticism on the work, and be specific.
- Credit other people's work and respect its licence.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting game files, keys or other material that can't legally be shared.

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the menu on the comment or post, or GitHub's [report abuse form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, and block people who break these rules. They apply this in the project's spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
